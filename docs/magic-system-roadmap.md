# Enfleshed magic system implementation backlog

The original Magic Runtime proposal is the destination for this system. Its initial V1 non-goals described what to defer at that time; they do not remove those capabilities from the eventual scope. Work should land in playable, testable increments while preserving server authority and data-driven definitions.

> **Active testbed override (2026-10-08):** All currently registered magic
> choices are available immediately. Cast-count discovery and Locus stat growth
> are disabled; historical implementation notes below describe how those
> mechanics were built, not the current test configuration. Locus minimum
> requirements do not gate casts or appear in the editor while progression is
> disabled. Server validation and safety caps remain active; development Lab
> casts also keep their existing resource-bypass behavior. This override does
> not claim that every proposal item is implemented; see the
> remaining-capability notes in the component matrix.

## Current foundation

The project already has a server-owned SpellSpec interpreter, registered and editable spells, Projectile/Beam/Wave/Field/Aura/Construct/SelfEnhancement/Manipulation Forms, Damage/DoT/Force/Heal/periodic Restoration/Shield/Slow/Bind/temporary MagicArmor modification/temporary model-scale Transformation/maintained physical Control/Enhancement/Manipulation/Summon payloads, bounded Space displacement, Elemental/Physical/Soul damage channels, Explode and projectile Homing/Pierce/Chain behavior, basic charge casting, temporary lifetimes, canonical server-owned Locus attributes with persisted current Mana/Strain and separate sandbox recovery tuning, a nine-slot Studio library, two summon archetypes with keyboard and on-screen Follow/Protect/Attack/Move/Patrol/Hold/Support/Dismiss commands, and procedural placeholder presentation. Spell damage continues through DamageService.

The Studio-gated spell library and current Locus Mana/Strain values now have separate versioned per-player DataStore profiles with validation and safe session fallback; authored Locus capacities and modifiers remain canonical server configuration. Space, Soul, and Time now have first executable expression/payload paths. Named special-resource pools are configured independently of Mana and Strain, authorable as spell costs and Resource-family conditions, checked and spent on the server, exposed through server-set player attributes, passively recovered from their registry definitions, and persisted with safe v1 profile migration. Focus is the current development pool; adding another pool requires a validated configuration entry rather than a new cost branch. A capability appearing in the editor is not counted as implemented unless its server behavior and useful feedback work too.

The runtime architecture guard in `tests/test_magic_no_spell_identity_branches.py` scans the Form, Payload, Trigger, Behavior, and service dispatch files to prevent mechanics from branching on proof-spell identity. Cooldown and lifecycle comparisons by `SpellId` remain valid identifiers.

**New composition coverage:** Beam impact is a server event at the raycast endpoint (including each Channeled pulse), separate from OnCast at the caster. Instant Beams can also use a generic bounded Cascade pattern: a deterministic server schedule scatters vertical sweeps around the resolved aim point, with each strike using world-obstructed hit detection and dispatching the ordinary Beam OnImpact. Compatible payloads can bind to each impact. Summon adds optional Event placement, so repeated impacts append bounded summons at their distinct contact positions; companions retain their authored role, assigned spell slot, source color, ordinary server cast validation, and command/lifetime lifecycle. This combines Beam Cascade and local Hunter summons without a Beam-specific Summon executor.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Target rules and conditions — in progress.** The bounded ALL/ANY/NOT condition tree and server-side cast/target evaluation are in place. EntityTarget supports Aim, server-acquired LockOn, AimOrLockOn, and projectile MaintainTarget (requires Homing and does not reacquire after target loss). Fields can be centered on a revalidated entity or exact raycast-validated world surface; the explicit `Area` targeting mode places a Field at a server-validated aim center without requiring an entity, object, or material source. Opt-in Field following keeps the area attached to that validated entity/surface and uses ordinary cleanup if the anchor is removed. Auras follow a revalidated entity or retain a local offset on a validated moving surface; Waves can orient their pulse toward a validated entity; Constructs can attach to a selected character when the placement sweep reaches that exact character unobstructed, or directly target an exact solid world surface under the crosshair. Object-targeted Fields/Auras do not consume material and reject character parts; moving area anchors expire and clean up when removed or made non-collidable. Target selection is capped by both TargetRules range and the actual reach of Projectile, Beam, Wave, or Construct geometry; target rules filter Any/Hostile/Ally/Neutral relationships consistently during target resolution. Spells may require target consent; a server-owned per-player session opt-in filters player characters from direct and area payload resolution, while NPCs remain eligible and spells without the requirement behave unchanged. Manipulation, Projectile, Beam, and Wave support ObjectTarget Force/Control on whitelisted movable parts without requiring a MagicMaterial source; each requires the exact unobstructed server ray hit and rechecks that the object remains movable, non-character, and tagged. The existing Raise operation also applies to ObjectTarget Projectile/Beam/Wave events when the exact part carries the matching material and MagicRaise authorization; anchored parts are allowed for Raise but not physics effects. Earth affinity can consume tagged Earth, Metal, or Wood; Water affinity can consume tagged Water. Compatible MaterialTarget Projectiles, Beams, Waves, Fields, Auras, and tagged Manipulation use these source profiles; successful casts consume the authored amount while the Form retains its normal geometry/placement behavior. Environment conditions expose server-resolved `Zone` and exact `ZoneTags` from tagged oriented BasePart volumes; spells can also declare cast-time grounded, clock-window, and required/forbidden zone-tag gates, validated in SpellSpec and enforced against server-resolved state. Status conditions now include bound/revealed state and the active MagicArmor bonus. Remaining: Studio acceptance of ObjectTarget selection and its ray/physics checks; SelfEnhancement remains self-targeted to preserve Body canon.
2. **Runtime lifecycle and commands — in progress.** Damage dealt/taken, health-threshold crossing, shield break, caster death, spell-caused target death, target death while affected by an active spell, sampled enter/exit events for Fields/Auras, bounded proximity events for traveling Projectiles and active Constructs/Self Enhancements, and typed conditional effect lifetimes are server-dispatched with bounded recursion. `OnInterval` is now a documented authoring alias for the existing bounded `OnTick` pulse path; old definitions remain unchanged. Active proximity samples use a configurable radius/interval, normal server target filters, one event per target, and a per-effect target cap. Aura and Construct can choose `UntilTargetLost` when following a validated character or object; following Fields opt in and homing Projectiles use `MaintainTarget`. Lost anchors run ordinary expiry, area-exit, or projectile cleanup. Sustained Field, Aura, SelfEnhancement, and Construct effects can also run `UntilResourceDepleted`, requiring positive ongoing mana upkeep and using normal server-side resource exhaustion cleanup. Projectile, Field, Aura, SelfEnhancement, Construct, and channeled Beam/Wave effects can use `UntilConditionFails`: the server requires the keep-alive predicate to be true at activation and expires through the normal cleanup path when it becomes false or cannot be evaluated. Condition-bound channels require an authored maximum duration; other condition-bound effects may also combine their predicate with a bounded maximum duration. Constructs, Fields, and Auras support `UntilDestroyed`; removing their authoritative model/marker runs the ordinary expiry and membership cleanup. Summon groups clean up on owner death/removal/respawn/disconnect, expiry, dismissal, summon death, external destruction, or Workspace removal, with per-instance listeners disconnected safely. Server-owned Follow, Protect, Attack, Move, Hold, Support, and Dismiss commands can be filtered by retained source-spell `OnCommand` triggers. Owners can cycle-select one active summon; a selection is validated against that owner and character before the command applies, while no selection preserves group-wide commands. Remaining: Studio multiplayer lifecycle acceptance, additional archetype behaviors, and lifetime cleanup across every remaining active component.
3. **Casting, motion, geometry, and projectile behavior — in progress.** Instant and hold/release Charged casts work, Projectile/Beam/Wave/Field/Aura/SelfEnhancement have bounded server-owned held channels, Projectile and Beam aim can update while held, Wave cones use full 3D aim, and Field/Aura/SelfEnhancement/Construct support toggle lifetimes. Field and Aura hit volumes support validated Sphere, vertically rounded Capsule, Cylinder, Box, directional Cone, annular Ring, thin vertical Plane/Wall, and forward Line corridor geometries; Box, Cone, Plane, and Line support bounded yaw rotation, with matching Magic Lab controls and local previews. Capsule defines radius and total height and uses exact server capsule-membership checks after a bounded box query; expansion scales both dimensions together. Fields and Auras can optionally grow or contract any supported geometry over time; the server uses the same interpolated dimensions for collision membership and the replicated area marker. Channeled Waves can grow or contract cone reach over channel age using the same validated interpolation helper, with matched server hit queries, object-target reach checks, telegraphs, and editor preview. Channeled Beams can grow or contract range and width together, keeping server sweeps, obstruction endpoints, object-target reach, replicated width, and editor preview synchronized; instant Beams remain unchanged. Constructs can also scale their server-owned collision model over active age; placement remains surface-flush, dimensional/volume bounds account for maximum growth, and server overlap plus axial ray checks pause expansion before it penetrates Parts, Humanoids, or Terrain. Custom Construct primitives may set independent, bounded lifetimes; the last part's expiry routes through normal Construct expiry and cleanup. Cone, Ring, Plane, Line, and Capsule Fields/Auras perform exact server-side membership tests after their broad-phase overlap query. A held Field can follow a validated character or surface while retaining ordinary expiry/exit cleanup if the anchor becomes invalid. Projectile supports Homing, Pierce, Chain, Bounce, Split, Spread, traveling Pulse, bounded Absorb and Reflect, a bounded post-impact Linger hazard, and a server-steered Return behavior that turns toward its living caster at a configured outbound distance and ends on catch or ordinary lifetime expiry. Return uses the existing swept hit path and cannot be composed with conflicting Homing, Linger, or Split steering/lifecycle behavior. A held Self Enhancement uses the ordinary aggregated movement modifier and ends on release, duration, or resource exhaustion. Cast windups can opt into damage/movement interruption. Projectile MotionPhases support up to four ordered, validated server-simulated segments with Linear, Arcing, Falling, Orbiting, Stationary, Expanding, or Attached motion. Arcing and orbiting offsets remain inside the ordinary swept collision path; stationary phases sample for targets at a bounded cadence; expanding phases grow hit and visible radii together, independently of the spell's initial appearance multiplier. An Attached phase requires Linger: on impact the bounded hazard follows the struck BasePart in its local frame and cleans itself up if that part leaves the world. Construct geometry supports Box, Sphere, Cylinder, and Wedge primitives, Arch/Pillar/Stairs/Bridge templates, and server-validated Custom compositions of up to five primitives; the same component registry drives editor preview and server collision. Remaining: broader Form geometry/motion coverage beyond this bounded set and Studio acceptance of attachment, growth, and independent component expiry behavior. Construct remains a discrete placement action rather than a repeated channel. Keep hit geometry server-owned and separate from appearance.
4. **Payload and control foundation — in progress.** Grow payload execution through typed registries, including damage-type/armor hooks, restoration, impairment, binding, displacement, detection, transformation and maintained Control. `Restoration` applies bounded recurring health recovery across Humanoid-target Forms and works as an ally-support spell for assigned companions. Constructs and Self Enhancements can also apply Heal, Restoration, and Shield to eligible nearby targets through bounded `OnProximity` sampling, enabling healing patches and protective zones without a separate support-form implementation. A bounded `ModifyAttribute` payload now supports temporary additive `MagicArmor` changes through the shared status-effect lifecycle; DamageService combines the active modifier with the target's base MagicArmor, and companion targeting can seek allies who need the armor buff. `Reveal` is a bounded Detection payload that sets an owner-scoped server expiry and renders a client-local through-wall outline only for the caster. `Displacement` now supports Space-authored directional relocation of valid Humanoid targets, with server-side path obstruction, grounded destination, vertical-change, and occupied-volume checks. Transformation V1 now supports a bounded temporary full-model scale change, stacks multiplicatively under a combined cap, and restores the original model scale on expiry, death, or cleanup; this is an executable first transform primitive, not a complete body-morph/rig-part authoring system. Constructs and Self Enhancements can apply direct Damage, DamageOverTime, Force, maintained Control, Slow, and Space Displacement to eligible nearby targets through their existing bounded `OnProximity` sampling path; execution still goes through the ordinary server payload and DamageService/control registries. Keep one-shot Manipulation distinct from ongoing Control. Optional mana/strain upkeep now applies to any active lifetime and ends effects cleanly on resource exhaustion; continue with richer payload/control types and termination rules without hidden side effects.
5. **Principles, expressions, and Locus resources — in progress.** Canonical Capacity, Efficiency, Potency, Strain tolerance, and ComplexityCapacity live on each server-owned resource state; current Mana and Strain load from and save to a separate validated profile. A shared Principle registry now represents Matter, Energy, Body, Soul, Space, and Time, names implemented expressions for all but the remaining future source expansions, and declares executable Principle/Form compatibility. Body now reaches every existing humanoid-target Form (Projectile, Beam, Wave, Field, Aura, Construct, and Self Enhancement) through the shared payload path; Soul, Space, and Time now also support Construct and Self Enhancement proximity effects using their existing payloads. World-material Manipulation stays Matter-focused. Magic Lab exposes all six and labels unsupported pairs rather than silently substituting a different source. Successful server-validated casts now advance bounded, configurable Locus practice ranks that raise all five canonical metrics; current resource values remain in their separate validated profile. Continue with richer expression-specific payload/target rules, ongoing upkeep, and broader material/environment requirements.
6. **Summon lifecycle and spell execution — in progress.** The bounded Wisp and Ward Sentinel archetypes share server-owned Follow, Protect, Attack, Move, two-waypoint Patrol, Hold, Support, and Dismiss orders, per-summon owner selection, crosshair hostile validation, and spell-slot assignment with independent cooldowns. Their model profiles and combat values are data-driven; both use the same authoritative target, assigned-spell, damage, and lifecycle paths. Companion spell casts pass through the owner's ordinary Locus cost, strain, complexity, and safety evaluation. Groups end on owner removal, expiry, dismissal, summon death, or external destruction. `UntilCommanded` groups now optionally survive owner death and rebind to the new character for up to 60 seconds; the default remains dismissal on death, and disconnect always cleans up. A dismissed group is suppressed across recurring Field/Aura pulses until that spell is successfully cast again. Summon payloads can select FixedDuration or UntilCommanded lifetime. The companion slot, role, lifetime, respawn policy, and spell definition are persisted together in the versioned Magic Lab library and restored to the server companion registry when the profile loads; only live summon models remain session-bound. Summon death, external destruction, or removal from Workspace immediately disconnects its health/death/ancestry listeners while leaving that group slot empty until expiry or dismissal, preventing pulse-driven replacement loops. Each server-owned summon replicates its assigned slot, selected target, and concise execution state (searching, casting, cooldown, blocked, out of range, owner busy, resource limit, or invalid assignment); the Studio debug display lists the owner's summon states, and cast rejection reasons flow back from the ordinary MagicService validator. Support target scans include player characters and explicitly tagged allied Humanoids; training dummies inherit their owner's relationship alignment, hostile tags still take precedence, and allied summons can receive support. Summon groups select a validated Guardian, Hunter, Medic, or Controller policy; Controller requires an assigned hostile spell with Slow, Bind, physical Control, or Displacement and never falls back to melee. Guardian preserves owner-radius protection, Hunter searches the full local hostile radius, and Medic requires an Ally-compatible companion spell and cannot fall back to damage. A persistent collapsed HUD now exposes all current orders through mouse/touch buttons and displays owned count, selected summon, role, companion-spell status, and server command feedback; the existing keyboard bindings remain. Remaining: Studio lifecycle acceptance, further archetype policies, and bespoke summon rig assets.
7. **Persistence, progression, presentation and audio — in progress.** The Studio Magic Lab's nine editable/equipped slots load and save through a versioned, server-validated profile. Current Mana and Strain also persist independently of the Magic Lab in a second versioned profile, with unsafe-profile protection, coalesced saves, and session fallback. Spell data has optional validated spatial cues for cast, charge, travel, impact, active effects, end, and break; blank IDs remain silent. Visual authoring now separates core shape/material, primary/secondary/accent colors, opacity/emission, particles, projectile trail, impact style, casting style, casting animation profile, and a bounded Projectile surface pulse from hit geometry. The pulse is cosmetic, aligned to world-hit normals, and uses the shared visual burst budget; server presentation consumes the validated profile and old Construct defaults remain Slate. Explicit particle budgets cap live emitters, continuous particle rate, and one-shot burst emissions without suppressing hit geometry or marker/beam telegraphs. The editor preview shows projectile appearance separately from the hit radius. Procedural poses now include data-driven Projectile, Beam, Ground, Self, Overhead, Ward, and Sweep profiles; windup/release gestures share an allowlisted pose registry, and charge poses hold their strength until release. Bounded Locus practice progression is now derived from validated successful-cast counts and exposed as server-owned player attributes; the authored rank curve is centralized in `MagicLocus.luau`. Remaining: Studio cross-session/multiplayer acceptance, richer gameplay discovery/unlock rules, and broader effect coverage for every Form. Preserve readable gameplay telegraphs regardless of customization.

## This implementation increment

Casting now includes a first-class `Toggle` mode for Forms that support `UntilToggledOff` (Field, Aura, SelfEnhancement, and Construct). Selecting Toggle authors the matching lifetime in Magic Lab; the server rejects Toggle definitions that do not use the compatible lifetime. The existing server-owned recast path remains responsible for stopping effects and running normal cleanup. `HoldAndRelease` now differs from `Charged`: both use bounded charge timing and server-validated release aim, while Charged auto-releases at its maximum and HoldAndRelease waits at full charge for key-up, then safely cancels if left prepared for eight seconds. The maximum wait is centralized in Magic Lab configuration. `CommandCast` is now an assignable companion spell mode for Projectile, Beam, Wave, Field, and Aura. It cannot be cast from a player slot; summons remain idle until an owner-issued Attack or Support order, then resolve targets, relationship rules, Locus costs, and spell effects through existing server paths. V1 limits each owner to one distinct active CommandCast execution (`MagicSummons.MaxActiveCommands`); projectile splits and spreads share the originating execution slot until every continuation expires.

`Sustained` holds one Construct in the world while the owner keeps its slot held, pays positive ongoing Mana/Strain, and ends on release or the required fixed maximum duration. It reuses server-owned construct upkeep and cleanup paths.

Locus resource calculations have moved out of the sandbox player test table into a pure resource model and canonical default profile. Each server-owned player state now carries Capacity, Efficiency, Potency, StrainTolerance, ComplexityCapacity, current Mana, and accumulated Strain. Efficiency scales upfront and channeled mana demand; strain tolerance scales the exertion burden; potency is used by payload execution; current mana and complexity can make a cast impossible; unsafe demand and projected overextension remain castable but are reported as unsafe. The sandbox retains only fast test recovery, initial reserve, and safe-threshold knobs. Current Mana and Strain load on join and persist on coalesced autosave, player removal, and server shutdown; respawn preserves their current values. A separate bounded practice-rank curve derives the five canonical metrics from successful server-validated casts; it is computed from the persisted discovery cast counters rather than client state.

Principle and expression metadata is centralized in `MagicPrincipleSpec`. The runtime/editor share one support table, including Earth/Water as Matter expressions, Fire/Lightning and the legacy Unawakened Energy marker, BodyEnhancement, Soul/Spirit, Space/SpatialFold, and Time/TemporalShift. Unsupported Forms are never silently reassigned. Displacement is restricted to Space; TimeDilation to Time. Soul-only damage uses separate `SoulResistance` and `SoulArmor` target attributes through the existing DamageService; elemental damage uses per-element resistance attributes. Water is available in the Element picker as a normal elemental damage channel and recognizes `MagicResistanceWater`; this does not add water-specific payload behavior or material physics. Water can also supply a generic Projectile from a tagged source; Manipulation remains Earth-only. Spell Element supplies presentation color and the ordinary elemental damage channel. This still does not imply status effects such as burning, freezing, or stun.

Sustained resource upkeep now shares the ordinary server-owned casting/lifetime path. Active spells may declare bounded mana demand and strain per second in their Casting data, independent of one-shot cost. Efficiency and StrainTolerance scale those rates; projectiles, Beams, Fields, Auras, Constructs, and Self Enhancements stop when upkeep can no longer be paid, with server feedback and cleanup. Channeled Beams use the same model as other sustained effects. Existing spells with no upkeep retain their prior behavior. Magic Lab displays the ongoing rates separately from cast demand, and validation rejects ongoing costs on instantaneous spells.

The condition data contract accepts bounded nested `ALL`, `ANY`, and `NOT` groups with typed, whitelisted properties across the proposal's condition families. Magic Lab now renders and edits nested groups recursively, with rule/group insertion, removal, inversion, and the same depth/node/child limits enforced by the server; its original checkbox rules remain compatible. Runtime ages, phases, and distinct targets-hit counts are exposed to the evaluator for active spells. Lifecycle events `OnDamageDealt`, `OnDamageTaken`, `OnTargetDeath`, and `OnCasterDeath` use existing trigger/payload execution with recursion bounded to eight nested reactions. This does not claim the remaining targeting modes, event set, command system, Locus progression, or persistence are complete.

Spell casting also supports the proposal's `RequiresFreeHand` rule. Equipment definitions declare occupied hands, character Humanoids may declare `MagicHandCount` (default two), delayed casts revalidate the requirement at resolution, and sustained channels end with server feedback if equipment changes leave no free hand. Existing spells default to no hand requirement.

The structured condition language now exposes `SpellState.IsMaintained`. It is true only while the server-owned effect is actively held as a channel, and is cleared before `OnExpire` conditions run. This lets authoring rules distinguish a maintained channel from an ordinary active duration without client state or a spell-specific branch.

Active spell lifetimes and Self Enhancements may include a typed `EndWhen` condition tree. For ordinary timed/toggled/resource lifetimes, a true `EndWhen` result ends the effect. `UntilConditionFails` reverses that contract: the condition must be true when cast and remains active only while it evaluates true; false or unavailable values expire it. This now includes channeled Beams and Waves, which retain a bounded maximum duration. The server evaluates only caster, resource, spell-state, environment, and time families, and routes completion through each Form's existing expiry cleanup. Instantaneous effects and target-dependent rules are excluded from this mode. Magic Lab provides a single-condition editor for supported leaf properties while the data contract accepts bounded nested ALL/ANY/NOT rules. This remains subject to Studio multiplayer acceptance.

Interrupted non-channeled Fields and Auras now use a common cleanup path when their caster is removed, invalidated, or reset: cached occupants receive `OnExpire`, then remaining membership receives `OnExit`, visual state is destroyed, and any channel reference is cleared. This avoids relying on another overlap query after the caster has disappeared. Other target-loss and destruction cases remain under review.

Structured target conditions now also expose `Target.HasLineOfSight`. Area target filtering performs at most one server raycast per candidate when either the target rule, legacy condition, or condition tree needs visibility, then shares that result with both the condition evaluator and target filter. This makes visibility composable inside bounded ALL/ANY/NOT rules without adding redundant raycasts.

Material-source compatibility now lives in `MagicMaterialSpec`: Earth-affinity spells may consume Earth, Metal, or Wood; Water-affinity spells may consume Water. Compatible Matter Projectiles, Beams, Waves, Fields, Auras, Constructs, and tagged Manipulation can select their source type in Magic Lab. The server only acquires live tagged Workspace sources with a finite sufficient `AvailableAmount`, rechecks the amount immediately before spending cast resources, and consumes the configured amount on successful casts. The Form retains its own placement and geometry; this is a material requirement/source cost, not physical extraction or material-shaped behavior. These profiles add no metal/wood-specific payload behavior.

Channeled Projectile, Beam, Wave, Field, and Aura use the equipped slot's number key as a hold control. The client sends bounded sequenced aim updates at the configured 10 Hz test cadence for Projectile/Beam channels; the server accepts them only for that player's active channel. Projectile channels fire a validated initial shot and then additional server-created projectiles at a bounded authored interval; each shot uses ordinary collision and impact handling, while the channel pays ongoing resource upkeep and reserves active-effect capacity. Field/Aura keep their authored position/follow behavior and reuse the shared area pulse/membership loop. Wave repeats its validated cone query through OnTick at a bounded interval. Releasing the same slot ends the active effect and runs OnExpire/OnExit cleanup. Death, mana exhaustion, or the fixed maximum duration also ends it. Channeling requires positive per-second mana upkeep; all five Forms use the same server lifetime budget and release path. Channels do not lock movement. Sustained channels for Construct/SelfEnhancement and general interruption policies remain unimplemented.

Constructs now support the existing `UntilToggledOff` lifetime contract. Recasting the same equipped Construct removes its owned geometry immediately; its ordinary fixed-duration mode is unchanged. This uses the existing per-player active-effect collection and no new timer or client authority.

Channeled Beam aim now updates while the number key is held. Magic Lab sends a sequenced update at a configurable 0.1-second interval; the server accepts only normalized finite directions for the requesting player's active Beam channel. Every pulse still resolves through the server sweep and physical world obstruction. Field/Aura channels do not send redundant aim updates.

Fields may use either world-point placement or a server-validated aimed Humanoid as the cast-time center. Auras may use Self or a server-validated aimed Humanoid as their follow center. This composes entity-target validation with relationship filters and area payloads, so an ally-only healing patch or Aura can follow an ally, and hostile area effects can follow eligible hostiles. Fields remain at their selected position after casting; entity-centered Auras follow the target until it dies or leaves Workspace, then expire and clean up occupants. Existing world-point Fields and self-following Auras are unchanged.

Waves may use `EntityTarget` in addition to their existing self-directed targeting. The selected or server-acquired character supplies the pulse direction after range, relationship, aim, and line-of-sight validation. Entity acquisition for Projectile, Beam, and Wave is capped by the smaller of the authored target range and that Form's maximum reach, so lock-on cannot select a character the actual geometry cannot reach. Wave cone hit detection now compares the full 3D aim vector rather than flattening it onto the ground plane, allowing upward/downward attacks while preserving the same server overlap, range, and obstruction rules. Horizontally aimed self-directed Waves retain their prior behavior.

Constructs may use `EntityTarget` to attach their temporary geometry to an aimed or lock-on character. Validation caps selection at the Construct placement distance; before spending resources, execution confirms the placement sweep actually attaches to that same Humanoid and rejects intervening surfaces or another character. Existing surface and free-space placement remain unchanged.

Projectile Linger transforms an impacting projectile into a stationary, bounded hazard using the existing projectile instance and active-effect budget. It samples a server overlap at its configured radius and interval, executes the projectile's OnTick payloads while active, and runs OnExpire once at duration end. Traveling Pulse uses the same event at a separate bounded radius/interval while the projectile moves. Absorb checks the bounded active-projectile list at a configured cadence, consumes only nearby hostile projectiles, and converts each interception into a capped potency increase for the absorber. Reflect redirects eligible hostile projectiles toward their original caster, transfers future damage/event ownership to the reflector, and caps both interceptions and reflections per projectile to prevent ping-pong. Both use the shared server-owned active-projectile list. Authoring rejects projectile OnTick triggers unless Pulse or Linger is enabled, preventing inert trigger configurations. This does not yet provide a distinct Area Form lifecycle or mobile lingering clouds.

The current entity-target increment lets the camera report the humanoid Model beneath the crosshair to a cast request. The server treats it as an untrusted hint, rechecks model membership, living Humanoid/root, caster exclusion, range, aim alignment, and configured line of sight, then aims the cast from its server-known character origin. LockOn policies query nearby eligible Humanoids and choose the most aligned candidate, with a bounded acquisition range and the spell's line-of-sight rule. MaintainTarget is limited to Homing Projectiles, retains the validated initial target, and does not reacquire another after target loss. Relationship filters share the same server classifier as condition-tree IsSelf/IsAlly/IsHostile/IsNeutral values; hostility tags take precedence over team/alignment, then mutual ally tags, with otherwise unclassified living models treated as neutral. This does not yet add entity selection for every Form or persistent player-facing lock-on state.

ObjectTarget uses the explicit `MagicManipulable` allowlist to avoid treating arbitrary map geometry as physics targets. The client reports the crosshair part, and the server verifies Workspace membership, solid/movable physics for Force/Control, lack of a Humanoid ancestor, exact ray hit, reach, and obstruction. Force/Control do not require a MagicMaterial source attribute. The existing Raise operation can also run on Projectile impact, Beam cast/ticks, and Wave cast/ticks when the exact selected part has a matching tagged material and `MagicRaise` authorization; anchored parts are permitted for Raise, while physics effects still require movable parts. Both physics payloads use the shared network-ownership lease path; maintained Control cleans up on target/caster loss or expiry. Construct ObjectTarget remains separately raycast-validated and can attach to solid non-Humanoid surfaces. Projectile, Beam, and Wave ObjectTarget still reject broad health/area payloads. Studio validation should check moving targets, cover obstruction during channels, authorized and unauthorized Raise targets, and that normal Humanoid-target spells remain unchanged.

Magic Lab's optional local geometry preview now distinguishes Projectile collision radius from independently scaled appearance radius, draws Beam hit volume, Wave cone boundaries, Field/Aura radii, Construct placement bounds, targeting range, and configured Explode radii. Preview parts and adornments are local, non-collidable/non-query, and short-lived; server validation and gameplay geometry are unchanged. Construct wall/floor contact offsets and the exact cast-hand origin remain approximations.

The current lifecycle increment records Humanoids actually passed to Damage, DamageOverTime, Heal, Shield, Slow, or Force payloads during active spell events. If another source later kills one of those targets before the originating active spell ends, its `OnTargetDeath` event runs once for that death, excluding the spell that supplied the killing damage to prevent duplicate reactions. Depleting an applied shield through incoming damage emits `OnBreak` for its source spell after damage resolution; expiry and cleanup do not count as breaks. Multiple pools depleted by one hit each emit once, and recursion uses the existing event-depth limit. Instantaneous spells do not retain target references after their execution.

Each `OnTargetDeath` trigger step can now filter for `KilledByThisSpell`, `DiedWhileAffected`, or `Either` (the backward-compatible default). Runtime source identity is tracked per active cast instance, so one cast no longer suppresses another active cast of the same spell definition from receiving its affected-target death event.

Energy's canonical expression registry now includes the proposal's `Force` expression beside Fire and Lightning. It uses the existing generic Force payload and does not add a spell-specific execution path or infer knockdown/status effects.

Summon groups now remove their wisps on owner Humanoid death, caster removal, character replacement, expiry, and disconnect. A caster death listener removes a group promptly, with a bounded heartbeat validity check as a fallback; per-wisp death and health-bar listeners are disconnected during model cleanup.

`ModifyAttribute` now applies a bounded, expiring additive modifier to an allowlisted numeric attribute. V1 exposes only `MagicArmor`: positive values reduce magic damage, negative values lower existing armor, and concurrent sources aggregate under a shared cap without overwriting the target's base attribute. The existing status manager expires and clears the modifier; the Magic Lab authoring path rejects unregistered attributes and values outside the cap. Wisp support targeting can select an ally who needs the assigned MagicArmor buff, while a negative modifier is classified as an offensive effect and requires hostile targeting.

Waves can use hold/release channel casting. A channel emits its ordinary `OnCast` pulse immediately, then repeats the server cone query through `OnTick` at the authored Wave interval until release, fixed lifetime end, or resource exhaustion. Projectile can also use hold/release channel casting: the first shot is immediate, later shots spawn at `Projectile.ChannelInterval`, and each resolves through the existing projectile behavior executor. Both expose local aim updates, bounded lifetime, owner resource upkeep, active-effect limits, and release cleanup. Ordinary Waves and Projectiles remain one-shot casts.

Body Self Enhancements can also be held as a bounded channel. The enhancement is applied through the existing movement aggregation path, remains active only while held, and ends on release, duration, caster loss, or resource exhaustion. Its ongoing Locus demand is charged once through the active channel path; the ordinary enhancement lifetime manager does not double-charge it. Construct remains a one-shot placement because repeating a held placement would create an uncontrolled stack of solid objects.

Cast data may opt into two bounded windup interruption rules: cancel if the caster takes health damage, or cancel after moving farther than an authored distance from the cast start. These apply to charged and server-timed gather phases, are disabled by default, and use the same server-owned cancellation/presentation cleanup path. `CanMoveWhileCasting` and a bounded `MovementPenalty` now apply through that same server movement aggregator during charge, gather, and held channels; sprint, Body Enhancement, slows, and dash validation continue to compose, and interruption/release/respawn restore the prior effective movement. `CanRotateWhileCasting` can freeze the gameplay mouse-look during those same phases; charged and gathered casts retain their server-validated starting aim, and the server rejects channel aim updates while rotation is locked. Channel release behavior is unchanged.

Projectile motion phases are optional data on a Projectile. Each timed segment may specify a speed multiplier, acceleration along current travel direction, and gravity; omitted motion values inherit the Projectile's base settings. At each server update, the phase clock advances through at most four validated segments, then the last phase remains in effect. New projectiles created by channels, spread, or split initialize from the same first phase; visual scaling does not affect collision. The bounded `Bezier` phase adds authored side and vertical offsets to a cubic path between its start and forward endpoint. Every update sweeps the actual previous-to-next curve segment through the ordinary server collision path, so fast curved travel does not bypass hit detection. Arbitrary per-target trajectories remain future work.

Maintained `Control` is a distinct physical payload alongside one-shot `Force`, movement-speed `Slow`, and explicit `Bind`. Control applies bounded horizontal pull or push acceleration for a configured duration, speed cap, and maximum target assembly mass; the server checks mass both when the effect is applied and while it remains active. It leaves normal movement input enabled, aggregates simultaneous influences under a server-side acceleration cap, and cleans up when the target, caster, or effect expires. Server network ownership uses shared leases so a short impulse cannot restore client ownership while another maintained magic force is still active. Bind is a separate, explicitly authored status: while active it prevents walking, jumping, and dashing, then restores the remaining movement modifiers when it expires. It does not add a general stun or status-effect framework.

The Projectile Form now has server-owned `Bounce`, `Split`, `Spread`, and `Pulse` travel behaviors. Bounce reflects velocity from solid world/terrain contacts up to a configured count and retains a bounded fraction of speed; character impacts continue through ordinary impact triggers and are never treated as surfaces to bounce from. Split replaces the impacting projectile with a capped fan of weaker child projectiles, excludes previously hit characters from their collision and chain-target set, and prevents recursive splitting. Spread creates a symmetric cast fan while retaining the aimed center projectile. Pulse samples a validated, server-filtered sphere around the moving projectile at its configured interval and dispatches `OnTick`; its hit radius is independent of projectile collision radius. The same `OnTick` trigger can continue through Linger after impact when both behaviors are selected. All additional projectiles count against the existing per-player/global active-effect limits and use validated component bounds. Homing, Pierce, and Chain behavior remain unchanged. Field and Aura Forms now also support persistent toggle lifetimes; the same spell cast turns the area off and runs its cleanup lifecycle.

`OnThreshold` steps are authored with unique integer target-health percentages from 1 through 99. When that spell's health damage crosses from above a configured percentage to that percentage or below, the matching step runs once for that crossing through the normal trigger/payload path. Healing and shield absorption do not trigger it. Separate thresholds can be authored on the same spell; crossing multiple thresholds in a single hit dispatches each crossed threshold once. It is not a persistent listener on every target and does not react to unrelated damage.

Projectile `OnProximity` requires the Proximity travel behavior. At its configured interval, the server resolves nearby humanoids through the existing overlap, relationship, condition, range, and line-of-sight filters, then fires the event once per target up to the per-projectile cap. It does not replace impact collision or add per-target Heartbeat connections.

Fields and Auras now sample valid occupants at their configured interval. A first sample fires `OnEnter`; a subsequent sample that no longer resolves an occupant fires `OnExit`. Ending the area emits exits for remaining occupants. Both use server overlap, relationship, condition, and line-of-sight filtering, and the editor only offers target payloads supported on those events. Sampling means enter/exit detection latency is bounded by the area's pulse interval.

Summons accept owner commands through the sequenced action remote. `Z` orders Follow (no autonomous attacks), `X` orders Protect (the existing automatic attack behavior constrained to hostiles near the owner), `C` orders Attack against the hostile model under the crosshair, `B` orders Move to a server-raycast-validated visible surface, and `J` sets two visible patrol waypoints (the second J press completes the loop), `H` orders Hold at each summon’s current position, `G` orders Support, and `V` dismisses all current groups. The persistent Summon HUD mirrors these orders with mouse/touch buttons, shows owned summon selection and server feedback, and shares its key hints with the input configuration. Move destinations are range-bounded and group members spread around the point. Fixed-duration groups clean themselves up; UntilCommanded groups remain until Dismiss. They may opt to survive owner respawn with a bounded wait; disconnect still cleans them up. Dismiss suppresses groups created by recurring Field/Aura pulses until the source spell is successfully cast again. Hold suppresses target acquisition and assigned-spell casts. Support requires a compatible assigned Ally-targeting spell and otherwise returns actionable feedback; while active it searches only for injured, unshielded, or otherwise eligible allied players. Attack commands validate ownership, living character state, hostile tagging, command reach, and line of sight; the Wisp approaches only while it can see its selected target. Assigned spells may contain combat or ally-support payloads; Charged spells use the ordinary server charge path and automatically release at their configured maximum. A shared policy rejects unsupported Forms/payloads, ambiguous mixed target rules, and support spells that could target hostiles. Wisps choose nearby hostile targets for combat spells or eligible allies for support spells, then let the ordinary server SpellSpec validator and Form/Payload executors perform the cast. The Controller policy additionally requires a control payload and prevents melee fallback. Existing slot assignment and cooldowns remain. Remaining: bespoke summon rigs and Studio multiplayer acceptance.

`OnCommand` dispatches through the ordinary trigger and payload registries when the owner gives an active summon group a server-validated order. The group retains its validated source definition for its bounded lifetime; each trigger may match any order or filter to Follow, Protect, Attack, Move, Patrol, Hold, or Support. Attack events target the validated hostile command target; other orders target the group's living Humanoids. Ordinary target and relationship rules still apply. An `OnCommand` trigger requires a Summon payload on another event so command-only definitions cannot be inert.

Companion spell execution now removes the Magic Lab-only `Development` bypass from an isolated copy of the assigned slot definition. The ordinary MagicService validation/spend path therefore charges the owner's session Locus resources and rejects casts that cannot meet cost, complexity, or safety rules; preparing the copy does not mutate the editable Magic Lab draft.

Field and Aura geometry now also supports an authored directional Cone. The Magic Lab exposes reach, full angle, vertical thickness, and yaw, and previews the outline. Server hit detection gathers candidates from a bounded oriented box, then checks each Humanoid root against the exact cone before relationship, condition, and line-of-sight rules execute. Cone geometry remains distinct from the Wave Form's transient cast cone and uses the same area lifecycle and payload triggers as Sphere, Cylinder, and Box.

Field and Aura also support a vertically oriented Capsule hit volume. Authors set radius and total height (at least the diameter); the broad-phase uses its enclosing box, while exact server membership uses the rounded capsule shape. The local and replicated previews show the body and rounded caps, and ordinary area growth scales the capsule dimensions together.

The `Reveal` Detection payload marks valid Humanoid targets for a bounded duration through the shared server status service. Reveal expiry and color are keyed to the caster's player ID, aggregated when that player reapplies a mark, and cleared with target/caster cleanup. A lightweight local controller draws an AlwaysOnTop Highlight only on the owning caster's client; other players do not render that reveal. It does not grant lock-on, damage, or target-rule bypasses.

Projectile `Propagate` is the impact-centered fan-out counterpart to sequential `Chain`. It can run on a character or world-surface impact, queries a bounded radius for nearby valid Humanoids, applies the same impact payloads with per-target falloff, and excludes already-hit targets. The server applies the spell's relationship, consent, condition, and line-of-sight filters and caps additional targets through `MagicLab.MaxPropagationTargets`; unlike Chain, Propagate does not choose a series of hops or render links. Studio multiplayer acceptance remains required.

### Place-persistent Constructs

Persistent is a distinct Construct lifetime for static, trigger-free world geometry. A versioned per-place DataStore record captures the bounded resolved primitives, transform, owner ID, and visual profile. Startup restores valid records; server destruction or removal deletes the record. The store enforces 128 records per place and eight per owner, rejects malformed profiles without overwriting them, and fails casts closed when DataStore access is unavailable. This does not synchronize new placements between already-running servers; they are restored by servers started after the save. Studio cross-session acceptance with API Services enabled remains required.
