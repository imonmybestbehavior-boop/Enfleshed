What this pass changes—and preserves

Existing silhouettes, texture alpha, atlas layouts, frame order, mesh vertex positions, UVs and triangles are retained. The revised texture view reads the actual baked PNGs; the original source PNGs are untouched. Soft utility effects keep their continuous falloff.

The mesh downloads are material-region variants of the existing GLBs. No new geometry is authored. The source view is a neutral kit-tinted reference, not a capture of a finished Studio spell. Neither side simulates the game’s lighting, bloom or all layered spell effects.
